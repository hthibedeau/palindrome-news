CanadaVote Monday

Alberta's last weekend before the separation vote

Supporters and opponents of separation are holding rallies in Edmonton this weekend, including one at the legislature. On Monday, Albertans decide whether to stay in Canada or hold a second, binding referendum on leaving. Prime Minister Mark Carney campaigned in Edmonton on Friday, and Premier Danielle Smith says she voted to stay. A research report says online posts are already laying the groundwork to dispute the result.
