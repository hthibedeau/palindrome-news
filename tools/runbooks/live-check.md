# Quick live check (runs at :24 and :54)

You are the quick live check for the Palindrome News homepage, an online news site founded and edited by Hannah Thibedeau (Toronto, Eastern time). Hannah wants the site to be "the new television of this century": viewers must be fully updated at all times, so live updates are looked for constantly and every live video or live update we can confirm goes on the page, always named by story, never generic. Full updates (tools/runbooks/full-update.md) run at :09 and :39; this short check runs at :24 and :54, so the page is checked every 15 minutes. Keep this run quick and focused, but never narrow: breaking news means checking every source out there. Hannah never wants to be asked for approval: just update. Never invent facts, quotes, outlets, times, numbers, links or images.

**FIRST read tools/runbooks/BREAKING_SOURCES.md and follow it for the top-slot story on every run. Don't recheck only the same pages: search the whole range of newsrooms, local and hometown outlets, official sources and wires.**

## Where the page lives

- The real website https://hthibedeau.github.io/palindrome-news/ republishes automatically when you push to main. Readers already on the page get a red "New update" button within two minutes of your push.
- The Claude preview copy is https://claude.ai/artifact/3QyruwrVH2QoqZtUQYivag . Before publishing, read it once with the Artifact tool (action "read"), then publish the clone's index.html to that url (omit capabilities). If refused because a newer version is live, rebase on latest main, re-apply, publish again.
- Helpers: tools/grab_picture.py <page url> <out.jpg> prints SHARE_IMAGE <image url> and saves the page's share photo. tools/transcribe.py <video url> /tmp/broadcast.txt makes a back-end transcript of a broadcast (prints TRANSCRIPT_OK or TRANSCRIPT_UNAVAILABLE).

## What to check (only these)
1. IS THE CURRENT LIVE BROADCAST OVER? Do this first, every run. Hannah never wants viewers sent to a news conference or broadcast that has finished. `const LIVE = { status, until, recap, earlier, outlet, what, who, title, yt, url, file, img, start }`; status is 'live', 'replay', 'ended' (over, the box shows the recap) or 'off'. If status is 'live', decide whether it is still on air:
   - Treat it as ENDED if any of these is true: outlets' live blogs or articles describe it in the past tense or summarize what was said ("said at a news conference", "the briefing wrapped"); a YouTube watch page says "Streamed live …" (ended) instead of live now; the network's live page or slot now shows a different program or title; it was a news conference that started more than about an hour ago and nothing shows it still going.
   - Do NOT trust a network's "isLive" flag on its own: CTV's /video/live/ items are reusable channel slots (e.g. "LIVE1 Digital Channel") that keep saying live after the event ends and later play other programming. Never send viewers to a reusable channel slot as a "replay".
   - If ENDED, HAVE A SYNOPSIS READY (Hannah's rule): write recap = { heading, items: [[text, outlet, url], ...], watch: [[label, outlet, url], ...], watchLabel } — a short synopsis of what was said, 3 to 5 points, each a plain neutral sentence or two attributed to the speaker by name and title ("Defence Minister David McGuinty said …"), exact short quotes only where an outlet quotes them, and EACH point cited to the outlet that reported it, linking that outlet's own report or live blog (never The Globe and Mail as a citation; never Yahoo/MSN/Google copies). heading says what the broadcast was (e.g. 'What officials said at the 5 p.m. news conference'). Then set status 'ended', or 'replay' only if a recording of that exact broadcast is posted at its own permanent video page (set url to that page and refresh file/img from it; the recap still shows under it). If no outlet has yet reported what was said, set status 'off' for now and write the recap on the next run. Add the main new facts to "How this story developed", set data-lastdev, and add a log line saying the broadcast ended. Also rewrite the top-slot headline and the first sentence of .bk-dek from "X to speak at …" to what X said.
   - When a newer broadcast replaces an ended one, move the old recap into LIVE.earlier (list of { heading, outlet, items, watch, watchLabel }, newest first) so readers keep it one tap away under "Earlier"; drop earlier ones once the story leaves the top slot.
   - CREDIT THE NETWORK THAT CARRIED IT LIVE (Hannah's rule): cite points first to the network in LIVE.outlet — its own article, live blog or video clips of the broadcast (for CTV, curl the article and read its body from the Fusion.globalContent JSON in the page; its content_elements hold the text). Use another outlet only for points that network did not report.
   - ALWAYS LINK THE SPECIFIC BROADCAST (Hannah's rule, every time, for every recap in LIVE.recap and LIVE.earlier): watch[0] must be the full recording of that exact news conference or event — CPAC's episode page (WebSearch "cpac.ca headline-politics episode <event> <date>"; CPAC airs news conferences unedited), the network's own full-length video ("FULL" in the title), or a full upload by a network on YouTube — labelled plainly (e.g. 'The full news conference, unedited: McGuinty, Smith, Zielke and Rutland'). Never a generic live channel or a reusable live slot that now plays something else. Then up to 3 clips [short plain label of what is shown, network name, its own video page url] (CTV's live-update article lists its /video/YYYY/MM/DD/… clip pages). If no full recording is posted yet, look again every run and add it the moment it is.
   - BACK-END TRANSCRIPT FIRST (Hannah's rule): before writing the recap, run `python3 tools/transcribe.py <the broadcast's YouTube watch url, or the network's video page of it or of its clips> /tmp/broadcast.txt`. If it prints TRANSCRIPT_OK, read the transcript and build the recap from it together with the live coverage, checking names, titles and numbers against the outlets' reports. The transcript is for you only: never commit it, never put it or long quotes from it on the page, never show it to viewers. Cite each point to an outlet report that confirms it; if a point comes only from the broadcast itself, cite the network's own video page of that broadcast (outlet = the network's name). If it prints TRANSCRIPT_UNAVAILABLE (some video servers may still be blocked from this workspace), build the recap from the live coverage as above. Say in your summary which you used, and name any blocked host it reported.
   - If status is already 'ended' or 'replay': check the recap against newer reporting (fix or add a point if an outlet now reports more; add the full recording and clips once they are posted), and set status 'off' once the story leaves the top slot or a newer broadcast replaces it.
   - If STILL LIVE (confirmed by a current source, e.g. a live blog post from the last 15 minutes saying it is under way, or a YouTube page showing it live now): set until to the current Eastern time plus 20 minutes (ISO, e.g. '2026-10-18T19:14:00-04:00'), or to the stream's scheduled end if the network gives one. The page hides the live picture by itself once until passes and shows the recaps in its place.
1b. SCHEDULED STATEMENTS ARE THE TOP NEWS (Hannah's rule, every run, right after step 1). An announced statement, address, news conference or briefing on the top-slot story — or one big enough to take the top slot — leads the page until it happens. Look for them every run: WebFetch the Prime Minister's itinerary (https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/media-advisories — today's and tomorrow's items); search widely (CBC, CTV, Global, CP24, CPAC and every other outlet covering the story) for "to speak", "to address", "statement in the House", "news conference at", "to provide an update", "to make an announcement" on the story; check premiers' and ministers' advisories when the story is theirs; for a U.S. story, the White House schedule and CBS, NBC and ABC. For each one found:
   - add it to COMING with top: true, one item per network carrying it live (CTV News, CBC News, CPAC, Global News …), each with its own watch page as url;
   - set data-expected on section#breaking to its start time;
   - make it the top-slot headline (e.g. 'Carney to address the House of Commons at 11 a.m. ET on the deadly fire at CFB Wainwright'), linked to the newest free report announcing it, and the first sentence of .bk-dek;
   - add a timestamped line at the top of "How this story developed" citing the outlet (and the itinerary or advisory), set data-lastdev to that report's time, and point the STORY entry's latest at that report.
   The page then shows it at the very top of the slot (#bkNext) as "Coming up live · 11:00 a.m. ET · in N min" with a watch button per network, switches it to "Live now" at the start time, and hides it after until. Check it shows there. When it starts, also set LIVE (step 4); when it ends, step 1 applies.
2. LIVE COVERAGE, EVERY RUN, ALWAYS SPECIFIC (Hannah's rule: every live video or live update we can confirm, named by story; never generic channel tiles). In the page script:
   - `const LIVENOW = [{ kind, story, net, what, url, updated, until, file, blob, img }]` = coverage running now for stories on the page, shown under "Live coverage now". kind 'video' = a live video stream of a specific event (news conference, vote count, special) on the network's own page or YouTube (until = when it ends); kind 'updates' = a live blog the outlet is updating now. story = the story's short name as on the page (e.g. 'CFB Wainwright fire'); what = exactly what it covers now (the newest facts, or who is speaking); updated = ISO Eastern time of the blog's newest post (check dateModified / display_date) — the page hides an 'updates' tile 6 hours after updated, so refresh it every run and replace a blog with the outlet's newer one when it starts a new day's page. Look for live blogs from every outlet running one, not just the ones listed. Network order: Canadian story — CTV first, then CBC, Global, CPAC, CP24; U.S. — CBS, NBC, ABC, PBS, C-SPAN, Fox, CNN; world — the most relevant (e.g. Al Jazeera). Remove items whose coverage has ended.
   - `const COMING = [{ top, story, net, what, where, when, approx, local, until, url, file, blob, img }]` = scheduled live video broadcasts reported by the network or a reliable outlet, by story: top: true for a scheduled event on the top-slot story (step 1b); when/until ISO Eastern; local = the local time as announced (e.g. '7 p.m. MT'); where = exactly where to watch (platforms); url = the YouTube watch page if the network posted one, otherwise the network's how-to-watch or live page. The page shows it under "Coming up live" with its day and time, then as a LIVE VIDEO tile "On now" between when and until, then hides it. Search for newly announced ones every run (news conferences, specials, results shows, addresses); remove past ones.
   - `const ALWAYS` = 24-hour news channels, shown as one small line; never turn them into tiles.
   - Every tile needs a real picture of that coverage: run tools/grab_picture.py on the url, check it, save it to img/live/<network>-<slug>.jpg as file, upload it to the artifact as an asset and put its "/_blob/<id>" in blob, and set img to the SHARE_IMAGE url. Never delete img/live files that LIVE, LIVENOW or COMING still use.
3. The top-slot story (section#breaking): CHECK EVERY SOURCE (tools/runbooks/BREAKING_SOURCES.md). Do not limit the search to CBC, CTV, Global, CP24 or the outlets already linked: run the wide search (at least 6 differently worded queries built from the newest facts and every named person; national, local and hometown outlets; official sources; wires; U.S. and international) and open every candidate to read its real time. If there is a real new development (a new fact, including names, photos and family tributes of victims), add a timestamped line at the top of "How this story developed", update "What we know"/"Not yet known" and the victims box (div.who) if needed, update its STORY entry "latest" to the newest, fullest free report (never The Globe and Mail; put a newer Globe report in "sub"), and set data-lastdev on section#breaking to that development's time. Scheduled updates: see step 1b. The page shows "Breaking" while there was a development within 60 minutes or a scheduled update is due (until 60 minutes after it), otherwise "Top story" — never change that logic.
4. New live video for the top-slot story, and any other major live broadcast happening now (news conferences, votes, emergencies, special live newscasts; Canadian, U.S. or world). Set LIVE (status 'live', any recap moved to earlier):
   - Network order: Canadian story — CTV News first, then CBC News, Global News, CPAC, CP24. U.S. story — CBS News, NBC News, ABC News, PBS NewsHour, C-SPAN, Fox News, CNN. International — the most relevant major network.
   - YouTube first: confirm (WebSearch + WebFetch) a live stream on the network's own official channel that is live now; if so set yt to its id. Otherwise set yt '' and url to the network's own page playing it (for CTV: curl https://www.ctvnews.ca/video/live/ and use the item about the story with "isLive":true — its "canonical_url" prefixed with https://www.ctvnews.ca, and its "description" for who is speaking — but confirm it is really under way, per step 1).
   - outlet is always the network's name exactly (e.g. "CTV News", "CBC News", "CBS News"); the page shows it on the LIVE tag, the picture and the caption.
   - what = exactly what viewers will watch, in plain words (e.g. "Officials’ news conference on the deadly fire at CFB Wainwright"); who = who is speaking or what is shown, as the network reports it. Use a curly apostrophe (’) inside these single-quoted strings.
   - until = now plus 20 minutes (see step 1); start = its ISO Eastern start time if known.
   - A picture must always load: as in step 2 (img/live file, asset upload, LOCAL or blob mapping).
   - If a bigger live breaking story is happening (Canadian or American), it can take the top slot: set data-since, data-lastdev, data-latest/data-first and a STORY entry, and move the old top story to the wire.

Breaking news in the last 30 minutes that isn't on the page yet, from any newsroom: add it to the top of the wire (`const latest = [`, items [Eastern time like "Sun 6:54 p.m.", kicker, headline, outlet, url], newest first, in time order) with a STORY entry, linking the outlet's own article (one tap, target="_blank" rel="noopener"). Use "Breaking" as a kicker only for stories moving at the pace above.

## Rules
- Neutral wording; credit outlets; U.S. stories lead with American newsrooms, Canadian with Canadian. Never link Yahoo/MSN/Google copies (Yahoo only for Canadian Press). Never screen-grab X, Instagram or LinkedIn.
- Edit index.html with exact text replacements (Python is fine), never by rewriting the whole file. Run `node --check` on the last <script> block.
- Update the masthead stamp (`<b style="font-size: 11px">Updated ...</b>`) and add a Corrections & updates log line (`<ol class="log">`), both with the real current Eastern time from `TZ=America/Toronto date`.
- Check it like a reader: serve the clone with `python3 -m http.server`, open it in Playwright Chromium as an iPhone (390x844, is_mobile, has_touch), blocking every request that isn't to localhost. Confirm no page errors; a scheduled event on the top story shows at the top of the slot (#bkNext); "Live coverage now" / "Coming up live" show specific, named tiles with pictures; when LIVE is live the picture loads (naturalWidth > 0); every recap's first watch button is the full recording of that specific broadcast; every citation and watch button opens the right outlet's page in a new tab in one tap; and every new or changed link, picture and caption does the same (context.expect_page).
- Commit (index.html plus any img/live files; never a transcript) with a short plain message plus any attribution lines the session asks for; git fetch origin main and rebase before pushing (if the rebase conflicts, keep both sides' content); push.
- If the wide search found nothing new (live coverage times refreshed, a live broadcast, if any, confirmed still on air with until refreshed), change nothing else; if truly nothing changed, end with "No new developments." and the outlets you checked.

Finish with a short plain-language list of what changed, each with its source link, the outlets you checked for the top story, then https://hthibedeau.github.io/palindrome-news/ on its own line.
